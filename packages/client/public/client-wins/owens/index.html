How this was run

Window

September 22 to October 22, 2026, which is 30 days, matching a monthly newsletter cycle. One strong item from just before the window is included and marked.

Dates

Every date comes from the source: the release URL, the post ID decoded on LinkedIn, the full timestamp on Facebook and Instagram, and the publish date on press stories. None comes from a relative label like "2w".

What counts

Awards, accreditation, rankings, grants, honors, milestones and positive press. Launches, campaigns, rebrands and website work get checked for who produced them before they appear here.

Sources

  • Owens newsroom, all 150 releases on the page
  • LinkedIn and Facebook, signed in
  • Instagram, signed out, which shows only the latest 12 posts
  • Google News, last 45 days
  • Reddit, r/Toledo posts and comments since September

Not verified

  • Whether the enrollment milestone already ran in October Insights.
  • The full text of the WTOL dental care story. Only its headline and summary were read.
  • The Facebook post count is approximate, because the page loads posts as you scroll.
  • Instagram posts from September 22 to October 7. Signed out, Instagram shows only the 12 most recent posts.
